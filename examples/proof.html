Viriditas · tint any text · hear Ecouter speak · the README

Each letter takes the colour of the pitch the voice gives it. The voice rests at 118 Hz, which is green. In Omnia, shown first, the voice's own range, an eighth of an octave up or down, is spread over the whole wheel: rising pitch runs through cyan and blue toward violet, falling pitch through yellow and red toward magenta, where the two ends meet. Viriditas is the quiet reading mode, one turn of the wheel per octave, so speech stays near green. Vivificat sits between. Same pitches; three ways to show them. Below, the voice sings your sentence, and every letter is set under the moment it is sung.

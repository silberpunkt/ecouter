A sonic lamp lights the tint hiding within text.

Here is how it works. Each letter takes the color of the tone the voice would give it, and the color is as strong as the tone is far from flat. Flat is green. A question lifts toward blue, an exclamation drops toward red. Omnia, shown first, spreads the voice's own range over the whole wheel, so the two ends meet in magenta; Viriditas, the quiet reading mode, turns the wheel once an octave and stays near green.

Flat is green. Up the wheel, cyan then blue, is a higher tone. Down the wheel, yellow then red, is a lower one. In Viriditas one turn is one octave; in Omnia an eighth of an octave each way is half a turn.

Background:

Units: Octaves from flat to full color. Auto reads the text.

How to use.

Write the thing. Tint it. Where it runs white for two lines, that is where someone would have liked to say "wait" and could not. Put a mark there. Not for grammar. For them.

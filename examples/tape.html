Tape

ecouter-player.js plays what Ecouter makes. The sound is a tape, and a head reads it: at any pace, either way, by itself or under your hand. The head lives on the audio thread, so nothing the page does can make it stutter.

Type a sentence and press Play. Then take hold of the picture and drag it: the voice goes with your hand, backward too. Move the pace while it plays and hear the tape slow, stop and turn round.

At the end:

The picture is the tape: its waveform, with a faint line where one sentence ends and the next begins. The red line is the head.

Options:
Voice: Eight voices, each asked of Ecouter by name.
Pace: The speed of the tape. Pitch goes with it, as on a tape. Below 0 is backward.
Voice's own: The speed this voice's tape is meant to be run at. Obelus is Arthur at 0.83.
Mellow: A room laid into the sound, on the tape.
Resound: A room after the head, in the air about the machine.
At the end: Stop, go round again, or turn back.

Two rooms. Mellow is on the tape, so it is dragged with the voice, and a room run backward is heard as one. Resound is after the head, and stays still. Try each alone with the pace below 0, and then both together: whether two rooms are one too many is a question for the ear.

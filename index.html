About

💡︎
What it is. Ecouter is a tiny English text-to-speech in one file. It was built to color text by the tone a voice would give it, once looked up. It does not have to sound out, but it can, rather well.
📖︎
How it reads. By the 355 letter-to-sound rules of NRL Report 7948 (1976), which have been public since 1985. The sentence is given a tune, and a small formant throat sings it. Nothing is recorded and nothing is looked up elsewhere.
🎨︎
The color. Each letter takes the color of the tone the voice would give it. Flat is green. A question lifts toward blue, an exclamation drops toward red. The same text gives the same colors every time.
🔔︎
A.M. Bell. Alexander Melville Bell drew Visible Speech in 1867 as pictures of the mouth, so that a sound could be read without having been heard, in any tongue. Here each of his signs is three keyboard characters. Choose an A.M. Bell sample and the Bell codes alone go in the box: Ecouter speaks them from the glyphs, through the same mouth, and colors them by the same tones. His forms can write any tongue. This voice has only English sounds yet, so the Spanish here is said by an English mouth.
◎︎
The rings. The library is the pearl: English in, samples out. About it are rings, each a small file of its own. The score ring works on a sentence before it is sung, and holds the voices by name. The player ring treats the sound as a tape, and reads it with a head. This page uses all three.
◑︎
High Contrast. If your system forces its own colors on pages, the tinted words still keep theirs, because there the colors are what is being said. At the very foot of the page a switch then appears: Yours, or The page's.
🔒︎
What it keeps. Your choices and your words, in this browser and nowhere else, so that they are here when you come back. Nothing is sent anywhere, and everything is made in your browser. Reset puts the choices back as they first were, and leaves your words alone.
◡︎
The sign. A lower hemicircle: an open mouth, speaking and perhaps smiling. Beside it the word, set in Eniee Slender and wearing its own coloring.
🔗︎
More. The proof that the colors are the pitch. The tint workbench. Say, with the wave and the spectrogram. Tape, the player alone. And the source, with its notes.

Howto

⌨︎
Write. Type in either box, or choose a Text Sample or an A.M. Bell sample. The lower box colors as you type. Sample - and + size the words in both.
🎨︎
Color. Choose a tint. Omnia spreads the voice's range over the whole wheel. Viriditas is the quiet reading mode and stays near green. Vivificat is between. A background lights the room behind the letters.
🗣︎
Choose a voice. Each is Ecouter, differently carried, and opens on its own four dials. Move them as you like. Voice's own puts them back.
▶︎
Say. The text is sung and played, from where the head stands. Reverse plays it backward. Pause rests the head where it is, and Stop sets it back at the start. Speed can be moved while it plays. The other dials sing it again.
✋︎
Take hold. Drag the playhead, the wave or the spectrogram. The voice goes with your hand, backward too.
✂︎
Take it apart. The finish is eight parts. Turn them off one by one and hear what the sound is at heart. It tries. It is basic.
💾︎
Save. A .wav of the tape, at the speed set. The room after the head is not in it.
